Deploy Schema Markup (FAQPage + SoftwareApplication + Organization)
layout.tsx → <head> — the single highest-leverage SEO/GEO move in the entire plan
Impact: High Effort: Low ~2 hours
Why it matters
🎯
Not one competitor (CaseEasy, Visto, VisaFlo) has deployed structured data. FAQPage schema gives you rich results in Google (accordion in SERP). SoftwareApplication schema tells every LLM — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — exactly what Formio is and who it's for. This is the #1 factor for GEO citation. Do this first.
What to build — File: src/app/layout.tsx

Add a JSON-LD script tag inside <head> with three schema objects. Replace the existing basic metadata export with full OpenGraph and Twitter tags too.

// src/app/layout.tsx — add to <head> <script type="application/ld+json" dangerouslySetInnerHTML={{ __html: JSON.stringify([ // 1.
